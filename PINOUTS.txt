A5 goes to BMP280 SCL and then gets chained into MCP4728 SCL
A4 goes to BMP280 SDA and then gets chained into MCP4728 SDA
One of the arduino GND's goes to all the GND on each component
The arduino's 3.3V goes to all the VCC on each component EXCEPT the Rev P 6 Wind Sensor

Rev P 6 Wind Sensor MUST have a separate 9V battery with it, GND of the battery (negative) goes to Rev P 6 GND, VCC of battery (positive) goes to Rev P 6 VCC
A7 goes to Rev P 6 Wind Sensor OUT (output)
